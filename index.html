Questions, answered

Is Workspaces really free?

Yes. The personal workspace is free with no sign-up. Paid plans only cover sync and team features that are still being built.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install it as an app and it keeps working without internet, saving your data on your own device.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose a future sync plan.

When will Pro and Teams launch?

They follow the roadmap above. Join a waitlist and you will hear first.
